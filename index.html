Speakers

Practical knowledge from people who work where our guests want to go, and on what keeps guides at their best.

Dr. Angelia SIA

Principal Researcher and Deputy Director, Centre for Science of Urban Nature, National Parks Board

Beyond Sightseeing: Connecting Visitors with Singapore’s Green Spaces

10.25am

About the talk and speaker

How can Singapore’s green spaces offer visitors more than a scenic stop? This talk explores Singapore’s green spaces, shares research findings on nature and wellbeing, and introduces the Nature Immersion Trail at Singapore Botanic Gardens, with fresh perspectives on helping visitors slow down, engage their senses and connect more deeply with nature.

Dr. Angelia SIA’s work explores how urban nature supports health, wellbeing and connection to nature, translating research into programmes supporting Singapore’s City in Nature vision. She holds a PhD from the National University of Singapore and an MBA from Imperial College. An ANFT-certified forest therapy guide and trail consultant, she bridges scientific evidence with practice to enrich nature immersion experiences.

Mr. GOH Ter Yang

Head, Singapore Conservation and Community Science, WWF-Singapore

Nature, conservation and responsible guiding

11.10am

About the talk and speaker

A glimpse into the nature (pun intended) of conservation work, and how tourist guides can make a meaningful contribution to protecting nature. Includes a preview of an upcoming nature workshop for guides.

Ter Yang has more than 15 years of experience in nature conservation and sustainability across a variety of sectors. He started as an educator, and his work grew to include stakeholder engagement and project management, including citizen science projects. At WWF-Singapore he leads the Education and Outreach team on engagement and conservation initiatives.

Mr. Raveen THEDJ

PEAK Performance Coach, Communication and Influence Trainer

How to Get CHOSEN, REMEMBERED and RECOMMENDED

12.20pm, during lunch

About the talk and speaker

A practical, interactive session on how tourist guides can stand out, communicate their value and create more opportunities in a competitive industry.

Raveen focuses on mindset, communication, confidence and professional performance. Using his PEAK framework (Purpose, Energy, Action and Knowledge), he equips professionals with practical strategies to communicate confidently, connect with people and improve their performance.

Dr. LIM Zi Yi

Medical Director and Founder, Centre for Clinical Haematology

The Long Journey: The Science of Living Longer, Living Better

1.50pm

About the talk and speaker

Lessons from medicine on health, ageing and resilience for tourist guides. Drawing on his experience walking alongside patients and their families, Dr. LIM shares what medicine has taught him about preserving healthspan, preparing for the major challenges of ageing, and the less obvious ingredients of living well, with practical ways to look after ourselves on long days outdoors.

Dr. LIM Zi Yi is the Medical Director and founder of the Centre for Clinical Haematology. He has more than two decades of experience as a haematologist, having worked at top haematology centres in London and Singapore. Dr. LIM is also the Medical Director and former President of the Bone Marrow Donor Programme. His present interests focus on the early factors that lead to blood disorders, including leukaemia, and how these disorders can be diagnosed and prevented.

Ms. Nicole LIANG

Assistant Director, Local Production Strategy and Development Division, Singapore Food Agency

Local farming in Singapore’s food resilience strategy

2.45pm

About the talk

How Singapore keeps its food supply safe and secure: the main threats to our food supply, the four pillars of Singapore Food Story 2, and a closer look at Grow Local. Learn how food is farmed in Singapore, why locally farmed produce matters, and where to find it.

Fireside chat, 1.00pm to 1.45pm

What the industry needs from tourist guides in 2027

Employability, service standards, and how guides, agencies, attractions and unions can work better together. With time for questions from the floor.

Good to know

Can I buy a ticket at the door?

No. Entry is by pre-purchased ticket only, and there are no walk-ins on the day. Ticket sales close on 24 November 2026, or earlier if seats run out.

Who can attend?

Licensed tourist guides, whether or not they are members of the Society, and representatives of industry organisations.

How do I receive a Statement of Attendance?

Choose a ticket that includes it, check in on Eventbrite on the day, and return the feedback form after the event.

Can I submit the talks for LEARN?

Talks delivered during this event can be submitted as Bucket C Physical Lectures under Cat 1 Tourism Content. As each bucket can only be submitted once, attending one or all talks counts as one LEARN.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Morning tea and a networking lunch are included in every ticket.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email us at info@societyoftouristguides.org.sg.
